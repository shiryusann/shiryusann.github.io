Welcome to my personal page! My name is Zilong /tsìlɔ́ŋ/ Li /lì/. I am interested in natural language processing and computational linguistics, especially focusing on formal/symbolic methods, human factors, and language technology for education. With a background in the humanities, I also enjoy discussing philosophy and world history.

## Research Interests

- **Computational Linguistics**: Computational phonology-morphology, computational semantics, and language parsing (AMR, dependency parsing, etc.)
- **Neuro-symbolic AI**: Formal reasoning and verification for LLMs, structured knowledge injection into neural models
- **Educational Technology**: Cognition-inspired educational AI, language technology for education, and multimodal analysis

## Research Experience

- **Student Researcher**, NSF National AI Institute for Student-AI Teaming (iSAT) at CU Boulder
  - *Multimodal Educational AI Assistant* (Oct 2024 – July 2025): Leveraged multimodal signals (gaze, pose) to evaluate students' behavioral engagement in class, incorporated evaluation results into the AI assistant for instructional support, and demonstrated the system at AAAI 2025 and the NSF site visit.
  - *AMR (Abstract Meaning Representation) Graphs Retrofitting* (July 2024 – Sept 2024): Maintained and updated the AMR graph knowledge base for information retrieval, enhancing the educational AI assistant's responses by integrating retrieved curricular materials.
